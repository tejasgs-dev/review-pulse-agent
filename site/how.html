How it works

From raw app reviews to a one-page weekly pulse, every week.

  1. STEP 1

    App reviews

    Public reviews are collected from the Play Store and App Store.

  2. STEP 2

    Filter & PII scrub

    Short, non-English and duplicate reviews are dropped, and emails, phone numbers and other personal details are removed.

  3. STEP 3

    ML theme clustering

    Reviews are turned into embeddings and grouped into up to five themes, like "Customer support".

  4. STEP 4

    Weekly pulse

    The top 3 themes, three real quotes and three action ideas are written into a one-page note.

  5. STEP 5

    MCP server

    A small server receives the pulse and handles publishing to Google.

  6. STEP 6

    Google Doc + Email

    The pulse is added to a shared Google Doc and sent as an email.

    Google DocEmail

Runs weekly

Each week, a GitHub Actions workflow runs the whole pipeline end to end.

Privacy first

Personal details are scrubbed before any analysis, and checked again before anything is published.

Grounded

Every quote is an exact excerpt from a real review.
